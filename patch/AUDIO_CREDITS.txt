FrontierHunts - audio credits and licences
==========================================
This is the only audio credits file. It lists the sounds this jar actually ships (full per-file table:
docs/AUDIO_ASSET_INVENTORY.csv in the source). The audio-provenance/ folder holds preparation records from earlier builds.
These licences apply only to the sound files named here. They are exceptions to the licence of the mod's code.
Attribution does not imply endorsement by any author listed. All recordings were edited for the game: trimmed,
channel-selected, filtered (high/low-pass, noise reduction or spectral gating), gain/loudness-matched, faded,
re-sequenced or pitch-adjusted where noted, and encoded as Ogg Vorbis.
