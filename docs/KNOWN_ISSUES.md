# Known issues

For 1.3.1, these items come from the original 1.3.1 release notes. They were not independently reproduced or marked fixed during the GitHub publishing review.

- **Herd navigation:** the recorded tests include followers lagging behind, slow regrouping after scattering, and a leader becoming stuck in dense pine forest.
- **Steep-terrain first hunt:** the recorded smoke test has three failures involving a deer above the player on a slope, scripted skinning, and claiming the reward. The explanation in the original notes is tentative, not a confirmed diagnosis.
- **Shooting sticks:** three resting checks were skipped because the test player could not ride. In-game verification is still needed for those cases.
- **Modpacks and shaders:** no claim is made that this release works with every combination. Include your mod list and shader when reporting a problem.

Publishing a new download link or updating the documentation does not resolve these gameplay issues. Reports should include reproduction steps and the exact installed version.

The 1.4.0 release notes also retain three steep-terrain first-hunt failures. Camera and shader visuals need testing with your setup. This publishing update does not change those existing issues.
