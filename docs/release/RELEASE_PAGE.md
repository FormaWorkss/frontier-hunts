# FrontierHunts

A hunting mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge). You track whitetail, elk and moose with wind, scent and sign. Place a
heart or lung shot, follow the blood trail and dress your game. It also adds a hunting season, contracts, the Ranger
Academy, camps, boats, deep snow and a wilder frontier world. Frontier Structures (villages and camps) is built in.

## Requirements

| | |
|---|---|
| Minecraft | **1.21.1** |
| Mod loader | **NeoForge 21.1.248 or newer** (21.1.x) |
| Java | 21 (bundled with the official launcher) |
| Install on | **Client and server.** Both must run the **same FrontierHunts version**. |
| Download size | about 206 MB (sculpted animals, realistic textures and recorded sounds) |

**Optional, supported:**
- **Sodium and Iris:** the mod never needs its own shader pack. Only the shader packs listed in the
  [performance guide](PERFORMANCE_GUIDE.md) have been tested with it; others may work but haven't been checked.
- **Distant Horizons:** Frontier landscape blocks are simplified in distant LODs, and optics range far terrain.
- **Xaero's World Map:** explored land is preloaded onto the map.

**Do not also install** a separate Frontier Structures jar. It is already inside FrontierHunts, and two copies stop the
game from loading.

## Installation

1. Install NeoForge 21.1.248 or newer for Minecraft 1.21.1.
2. Put `FrontierHunts-<version>.jar` in your `mods` folder.
3. Start the game and open **Frontier Hunts settings**: Options → Video Settings → **Frontier Hunts…** (top left),
   or bind a key under Controls. Choose the **Vanilla** or **Ultra** preset.
4. Create a new world. The **Frontier — Alpine Wilderness** or **Frontier Hunts** world type (More World Options →
   World Type) gives the full reserve: its landscape, wildlife and regions. In ordinary
   worlds whitetails still roam forests, plains and meadows (config `whitetailsOutsideReserve`).

New players get a **Frontier Handbook**, a starter bow and 3 arrows. The Handbook walks you through your first
hunt, step by step.

## Multiplayer

- Put the **same jar** on the server and on every client. A mismatched version can't join.
- Server settings (realism, regulations, survival, seasons, weather) are in `config/frontierhunts-server.toml`, or
  in the settings screen for operators.
- **`/locate biome`** answers in moments for most biomes. Very rare ones may take up to 10 seconds, after which it
  stops and reports that none was found nearby. It can no longer stall the server long enough for the watchdog
  (fixed in 1.2.6).

## Known issues

- **Bison, boar, coyote, wolf, cougar, pronghorn, grizzly, black bear, polar bear, lion, panther and cheetah are
  previews.** They have spawn eggs but don't spawn on their own yet, and have no contracts.
- **Distant Horizons and scopes:** past your render distance, a scope shows Distant Horizons' terrain, not loaded
  blocks. Animals out to 640 blocks are still drawn and can be ranged.
- **Iris with Distant Horizons:** the log may show `Unexpected; somehow the Opaque + Translucent pass ran with shaders
  on` once when you join a world. It is harmless, and happens on the frame Distant Horizons switches *Fabulous*
  graphics off. Use *Fancy* graphics with shaders to avoid it.
- **World changes apply to new land only.** Villages generated by older versions keep their old terrain.
- **English only** for now.

## Comfort and accessibility

Open **Frontier Hunts settings → Interface & HUD → Comfort**:
- **Camera shake:** 0–100%. It covers recoil kick, deep-snow lurch and shivering.
- **Blood effects:** Full, Reduced or Off. The blood trail on the ground always stays, because it is how you track.
- **Reduced motion:** calmer menus and cameras, and no kill cam.

The same page also has **Kill cam** (Off, lethal shots or trophies only; X-ray on or off) and **Arrival cards**. On the
**Wildlife** page you can set **Bird hit effects**, and the **Effects** page has **Waterfall detail** and **ATV screen
splatter**.

## Performance

See the **Performance guide** for measured frame rates and the settings that matter most. Short version:
- Start with the Ultra preset.
- On a weaker PC, open **Performance → Tune Ultra for your PC** and lower *Distant trees* and *Realistic detail*.
- Waterfall detail and storm effects cost the most in busy scenes.
